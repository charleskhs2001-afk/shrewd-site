What we collect

  • What you search for. We use it to find prices and keep results for up to an hour so repeat searches are quick. It is not linked to your name.
  • Payments. Payments are taken by Stripe. We never see or store your card details. We keep a reference to your payment in a cookie on your device so we know what you have unlocked.
  • Technical logs. Our hosting provider records basic request information, such as IP address and time, to keep the site running and secure.

What stays on your device

  • Your Christmas list and recent searches are saved in your browser only. We cannot see them. Clearing your browser data removes them.
  • Access cookie: remembers deals you have unlocked or your membership. Strictly necessary for what you paid for.
  • Your advert cookie choice: remembers whether you accepted or rejected advertising cookies.

Adverts

We show adverts from Google to keep searching free. Google only sets advertising cookies if you choose Accept. If you choose Reject, no adverts are loaded. You can change your choice by clearing this site's data in your browser. Google's own policy explains how it uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
