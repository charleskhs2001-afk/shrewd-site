    Questions

    Good to know

    Is it really free to search?

    Yes. Searching, comparing and seeing every price is free, with no sign-up. You only pay if you choose to unlock the cheapest shop.

    Which shops do you include?

    Established UK retailers such as Argos, John Lewis, Boots, Currys and Amazon UK, plus UK businesses whose identity has been verified. We never include open marketplaces such as eBay, Temu or AliExpress, or overseas drop-shippers. Each price says which kind of seller it is.

    How accurate are the prices?

    Where a shop allows it, we check the price on its own website at the moment you search; those prices say "Checked at shop". Some shops block automatic checks, so their price is the latest one they have listed and says "Listed price". Delivery costs are not included. Always confirm the price on the shop's site before you buy.

    Do shops pay to appear or to be ranked higher?

    No. Shops are ordered by price, cheapest first, and nothing else.

    Will it arrive before Christmas?

    Each shop sets its own last order date for Christmas delivery. Check it on the shop's website before you order. We are not the seller, so we cannot guarantee stock or delivery.

    What is the Christmas list?

    Tap "Add to list" on any product to save it, with its lowest price. Your list adds up the total and can be shared. It is kept on your device only.
